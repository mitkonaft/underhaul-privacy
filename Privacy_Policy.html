Overview

Underhaul is a single-player, offline mobile game developed by Dmitrii Kabanov. You can play its core features without creating an account, signing in, or connecting to the internet.

Information Collection and Use

Underhaul does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay telemetry, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. The game does not require your name, email address, or location to play.

Game progress, save files, and preferences are stored locally on your device so the game can remember your progress and settings. Dmitrii Kabanov does not receive or access those local records through the game.

Local Saves, Retention, and Your Choices

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it using an available in-game save-management option, clear app data through device controls, or uninstall Underhaul. The game has no server-side account or game-data record for us to retrieve or delete. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your operating system or backup provider and may retain a separate copy under their settings.

You may contact us at any time about this policy. If you voluntarily send an email, we receive the address and message you provide solely to read and respond to your request. Email correspondence is separate from in-game data and is retained only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable obligations.

Third-Party Access

Underhaul does not include third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services, and it does not share local game saves with third parties. Apple may process information related to downloading the app or using iOS services under Apple’s own privacy practices; those activities are separate from data collection by Underhaul.

If you choose to contact us by email, your email service and our email provider may process that communication to deliver it.

Children's Privacy

Underhaul does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. Because the game does not require an account or collect personal data, the same in-app data practices apply to all players. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so we can address the request.

Security

Game saves and settings are kept on your device and are subject to your device’s security and backup settings. We do not operate a game server that stores your progress.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the game or its data practices change. The updated policy will show a new effective date. If a future version begins collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before that change applies.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Dmitrii Kabanov at mitkonaft@gmail.com.
